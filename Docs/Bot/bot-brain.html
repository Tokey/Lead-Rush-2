    Chapter 2.5 · Dual-Layer Pathfinding

    Strategic spatial scoring + decoupled NavMesh steering

    LeadRush 2 splits movement into two cooperating layers: Layer 1 (TacticalBotBrain, ~4 Hz) scores candidate CoverPoint, TacticalPoint, FlagAnchor, or leader-offset world coordinates; Layer 2 (BotController, every frame) drives a decoupled NavMeshAgent (updatePosition = false) with 0.6 s / 1 m repath hysteresis, slices corners ahead on path.corners[1], blends combat ADAD strafing (0.65·route + 0.85·right·dir), completes OffMeshLinks, and triggers 1.1 s obstacle hops / 2.4 s ResetPath() stuck recovery.

    Chapter 3 · Motor Control

    Fitts's law flick + damped-spring pursuit

    Turns > 10.5° trigger a ballistic primary submovement shaped by Flash & Hogan's minimum-jerk quintic (10u³ - 15u⁴ + 6u⁵), exited at u ≥ 1 || angularDist < 2.2° followed by a 110–165 ms visual feedback refractory window. Between flicks, a closed-loop spring (ω = aimSpeed, ζ = 0.8 fighting) + dual-axis Perlin sway steers via pure delta mouse inputs capped at 720°/s.

    Tab 2 · Game Rules · MatchManager.cs & MatchSettings.cs

    Four game modes & how bots adapt per ruleset

    Every match runs through MatchManager.cs with customizable ModeRuleConfig rules (score limit 1–100, time limit 60–1800 s, respawn delay, optional seeker drones, per-mode difficulty override) and four selectable modes from the Main Menu across all 4 maps (Saltworks, Deadsector, Foundry, TestRange).

    1v1 Duel

    TeamId.None · 1 Rival

    Head-to-head adaptive duel. First to 15 kills wins; enemyBots forced to 1, friendlyBots = 0. Bots track your individual accuracy, crouch rate, and camp timer every fight.

    Free-For-All (FFA)

    Everyone Hostile

    Up to 8 independent bots on TeamId.None fighting player and each other. Smart spawn scoring maximizes min-distance from all live threats; bots investigate third-party gunfire.

    Team Deathmatch (TDM)

    Alpha vs Bravo

    Player leads TeamId.Alpha with 0–5 friendly bots against Bravo. Friendly bots run Step 7 FollowLeader staggered 2.5–5 m wide, 4–8 m back, watching ±75° flank sectors, and answer [T] chat orders.

    Rotating CTF (Team + FFA B51)

    BF6 Strategic Zone
