# P4 - Color-blind-safe hitmarker colors

LR2 currently uses white for body hits, orange for headshots, and red for kills,
with a black outline and a skull/zone label. This is mostly shape-plus-text
safe, but the orange/red kill colors can collapse under red-green and blue-yellow
color-vision deficiencies and can blend into warm map scenery.

## Palette requirements

Use separate luminance and shape cues, not hue alone:

- Body hit: white or near-white, strong black outline.
- Head hit: cyan or a yellow-cyan combination, keep a `HEAD`/`HEADSHOT` text label.
- Kill: magenta or saturated blue-white? Prefer a high-luminance cyan-to-white
  kill marker plus a skull and `KILL`/headshot label. Do not use red alone.
- Keep one 1.4 px minimum black outline around every hitmarker arm.
- Preserve at least 3:1 contrast against the dark outline and the sampled map
  background. Critical small text should preserve 4.5:1 against local backing.

## Proposed candidate palette

The UI owner must run an actual simulator/checker against screenshots and use
the result rather than a theoretical swatch:

| State | Candidate sRGB | Relative luminance | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | `#FFFFFF` | 1.000 | Existing shape+outline |
| Head | `#FFD27A` | approx. 0.66 | Warm, but lower hue reliance through shape/text |
| Kill | `#FF4D7A` | approx. 0.27 | Pink/magenta, stronger distinction under deuteranopia |
| Alternative kill | `#64D8FF` | approx. 0.55 | Cyan remains bright and distinct from warm hit color |

The final kill/head colors must be chosen by screenshot checker because map
brightness and HDRP tonemapping change perceived contrast.

## Acceptance

1. Validate body/head/kill swatches and screenshots in protanopia, deuteranopia,
   tritanopia, and monochrome simulations.
2. Use 3:1 minimum for graphical hitmarker elements and 4.5:1 for critical text
   against its immediate backing.
3. Keep body/head/kill distinguishable by shape or explicit label:
   X is body/head; skull or cross shape is kill; zone text names the hit.
4. Test on Foundry, Saltworks, Deadsector, and TestRange at 1080p and 720p.
