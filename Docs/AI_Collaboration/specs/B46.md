# B46: Four-map competitive flow pass

## Research basis

The design uses readable three-lane decisions, alternating hard cover and soft
cover, and a safe route out of every spawn. Sightline targets are based on the
engagement ranges of the weapon roster rather than an arbitrary open arena.

Sources:

- [GDC Level Design Workshop](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1025684/Level-Design-Workshop-Vol-1)
  (learnability and readable spaces).
- [Valve, Team Fortress 2 level design](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Team_Fortress_2_Level_Design)
  (lanes, routes and class-readable combat spaces).
- [Riot, VALORANT map design](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/ask-valorant-answers-1/)
  (chokepoints, timing and attacker/defender information).
- [GameDeveloper, spawn-point design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/spawn-points-in-multiplayer-games)
  (spawn safety and preventing immediate unavoidable deaths).

## Shared measurable targets

- Three primary lanes plus one connector/flank per map; every objective has two
  attack routes and one rotation route.
- Intended sightline lengths: SMG/pistol 8-20 m, assault rifle 20-40 m, FAL
  30-55 m. No spawn-to-spawn line of sight.
- Place hard cover every 8-12 m on a contested lane, with a second piece within
  3 m of the first so a player can cross while reacting. Avoid cover chains that
  make a head-glitch every 2 m.
- First contact target is 6-10 s after spawn (walking, not sprinting); objective
  capture route target is 18-25 s. A spawn must have two exits and 1.5 s of
  protected movement before an enemy can see it.
- One elevated position per side is enough for overwatch; each must have a
  readable flank and no view of both enemy exits simultaneously.

## Per-map changes for Gemini

### Saltworks

Keep the harbour as the close/medium map: label **Crane**, **Plaza** and
**Warehouse** lanes. Break the long plaza view with waist-high cargo cover at
10 m and 20 m, preserve the raised warehouse catwalk as the only FAL perch, and
add a covered side route behind the tanks. Move/rotate spawns so neither can see
the opposing ramp; target first contact 7-9 s.

### Deadsector

Use the ruined street as the rifle lane and the interiors as SMG lanes. Add a
crossing cover piece every 10 m in the street, open one rubble flank between
buildings, and block upper-floor windows that see both exits. Give each spawn an
immediate doorway plus a second alley exit; target first contact 8-10 s.

### Foundry

Retain the vertical trench, but cap the upper platform's view to one lane with
occluder panels. Add a stair-side flank and a low pipe crossing every 8-12 m.
Place a safe landing/retreat pocket below each stair so falling or rotating does
not expose a player to both teams; target first contact 6-8 s.

### TestRange

Turn the test arena into three measurement lanes: 12 m SMG, 30 m AR and 45 m
FAL. Add identical cover gates at 8, 16 and 24 m, a symmetric side flank, and
spawn walls that block the first 12 m. Keep a clean centerline for QA and make
the elevated platform one-lane-only; target first contact 6-8 s.

## QA before/after

For each map, record nav-walk time to first enemy visibility from every spawn,
minimum spawn-to-enemy ray distance, lane sightline histogram by weapon class,
cover gaps, and deaths in the first 3 s. Reject a change if a spawn has an
unavoidable line of sight, first contact is under 6 s, or any intended class has
no lane in its target range.
