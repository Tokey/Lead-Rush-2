# P2 - Bullet-hole decals per surface material

LR2 already has the core impact pipeline: `SurfaceIdentifier` marks material
type, `SurfaceLibrary` resolves impact prefab/decal/sound/size, and
`ImpactSystem` places an HDRP `DecalProjector` in a 128-slot ring buffer.
The remaining gap is visual and physical correctness across every surface.

## Surface variants

| Surface | Decal character | Impact character | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metal | Dark penetration scar, edge deformation, small spark dust | High short flash + sharp ring | Preserve metallic/smoothness response |
| Concrete | Gray chipped rim, powder halo | Dust puff and low-contrast chip | Avoid a black hole on light walls |
| Wood | Dark grain cut, splinter fringe | Small splinters; less dust | Rotate to align with grain where practical |
| Dirt | Compact crater/soil bruise | Dust cloud; usually no hard hole ring | Decal optional |
| Glass | Radial/branch crack plus star hole | Shards/sparkless impact | Use `LR2GlassMaterials` only on opaque/receive-decal panes |
| Water | Ripple/disturbance or no hole | Splash ring | No permanent decal |
| Fabric | Soft puncture with fray | Minor lint/dust | No metal-like mask response |
| Plastic | Small melted rim, subtle deformation | Sparse particles | Lower smoothness locally |

## Technical targets

1. Keep each surface decal in its own material/texture set, not one gray
   bullet-hole texture globally recolored by the surface library.
2. Use the existing ring buffer, 60 m draw distance, 128 max decals, and
   surface-normal offset. Generate deterministic random rotation per hit.
3. Align the projector normal with `-surface normal`, offset 0.02 m toward the
   surface, and cap depth at 0.12 m. Reject water/glass? Glass should use the
   cracked-glass normal treatment without projecting decals on transparent
   glass if HDRP cannot receive them.
4. Affect albedo and material mask on metal/concrete; avoid normal and emissive
   override unless a tested surface needs it.
5. Keep total decal cost stable at the configured ring size. Decal meshes should
   remain instanced; do not replace the ring with persistent per-hit objects.

## Acceptance

- Before/after screenshots of each surface on the TestRange.
- Metal, concrete, wood, dirt, glass and water all produce the specified
  `SurfaceType` behavior.
- Decals stay within 60 m and no persistent GameObject growth above ring max.
- Both assemblies compile; no HDRP decal/material errors.
