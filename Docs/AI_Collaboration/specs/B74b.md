# B74b — Disengaged Bot Resupply at Ammo Stations

## Goal
Bots seek out and use mid-map `AmmoCrate` stations when low on ammo or when their armour is broken (`Health.Armor` / `Health.HasArmor` / `Health.ArmorBroken`), without ever interrupting an active firefight. Because each `AmmoCrate` refills 120 rounds and 50 armour on a shared 20 s cooldown (`AmmoCrate.All` / `AmmoCrate.IsReady`), stations become naturally contested map objectives between engagements.

## Research & Design Rationale
1. **Strict combat preemption (Orkin, *Three States and a Plan: The A.I. of F.E.A.R.*, GDC 2006):**
   Maintenance and resource-seeking goals must never override survival or live-target engagement. Turning away from a visible hostile or leaving cover while under fire is a classic FPS bot defect that creates free kills. Resupply is therefore strictly **disengaged-only**:
   - Blocked whenever `targetVisible`, `underFire`, `recentlyDamaged`, or `TimeSinceSeen <= 2.5f`.
   - Never overrides `Engage`, `Retreat`, `TakeCover`, `Flank`, `Hunt`, `Investigate`, active non-`FreeRoam` directives, or urgent CTF defense (`MoveTo` toward a contested flag).
2. **Utility gating & hysteresis on shared-cooldown pickups (Mark, *Behavioral Mathematics for Game AI*, 2009):**
   - **Armour broken trigger:** `Health.HasArmor && (_armorBroken || Health.Armor <= 0.001f)` tracked via `Health.ArmorBroken` and cleared on `Health.ArmorRestored`, `OnRespawned`, or whenever `Armor > 0.001f`.
   - **Low-ammo trigger:** active firearm has `ReserveAmmo <= Mathf.Max(12, MagazineSize)` (roughly one spare magazine or less) and `AmmoInMagazine + ReserveAmmo < MagazineSize + startingReserve`, so the bot still carries enough rounds to fight if ambushed on the way to the station.
   - **Shared cooldown awareness:** candidate selection filters `AmmoCrate.All` by `crate != null && crate.gameObject.activeInHierarchy && crate.IsReady` within `85 m` and samples a reachable NavMesh point within the station's `1.8 m` trigger footprint. If another combatant claims the station while the bot is en route (`!crate.IsReady`), the bot immediately drops `_resupplyCrate` and yields back to normal brain goals (`Patrol` / `FollowLeader` / `ObjectiveFlag`).

## LR2 Implementation Plan
1. `Assets/LR2/Runtime/AI/BotTypes.cs`:
   - Add `Resupply` to `BotGoal`.
   - Add `armor01`, `hasArmor`, `armorBroken`, `reserveAmmo`, `lowAmmo` to `BotBlackboard` (plus `armor` and `reserveAmmo` in `Snapshot` for debugging/telemetry).
2. `Assets/LR2/Runtime/AI/BotController.cs`:
   - Subscribe to `Soldier.Health.ArmorBroken` and `Soldier.Health.ArmorRestored` in `OnEnable`/`OnDisable`; reset `_armorBroken` and `_resupplyCrate` in `OnRespawned`.
   - Populate armour and reserve-ammo state in `UpdateBlackboard`.
   - Evaluate a disengaged-only resupply goal (`ApplyDisengagedResupplyGoal`) right after `Brain.Think` when the bot needs ammo or armour, is completely disengaged, and the brain returned a non-combat idle/patrol/follow/non-urgent-move intent.
   - Add a small static/instance helper `ShouldSeekResupply` and `TryPickReadyAmmoCrate` so QA checks can verify both the mid-fight suppression invariant and the disengaged resupply selection.
3. Verify `dotnet build LR2.Runtime.csproj` and `dotnet build LR2.Editor.csproj` pass with 0 warnings/0 errors.
