# B75 — Props, Assets & Surface Wear Overhaul

## Goal
Replace remaining primitive (`Cube` / `Cylinder`) tactical cover, interior furniture, quay bollards, utility structures, skyline silhouettes, and `LR2_AmmoCrate` geometry with the HDRP-ready pack meshes already present in `Assets/LeartesStudios/` and `Assets/ScansFactory/`, while adding surface detail breakup, seam/edge wear decals, and grounded contact cues without altering cover heights, NavMesh traversal, or `B1` enemy readability.

## Research & Design Rationale
1. **Silhouette & Micro-Surface Breakup Over Raw Polygon Count (McGuire / Polycount / GDC *Material & Environment Art*):**
   Replacing flat 6-face primitives with bevelled, profiled meshes (barrels, wooden/metal crates, tables, chairs, industrial pipes, Jersey barriers, concrete columns, cable spools) dramatically improves grazing specular highlights, contact shadows, and human-scale depth perception (`specs/B42.md` Rank #1–#5).
2. **Grounded Transition Decals & Accumulation of Use (Unity HDRP Decal Projector docs; *An Approach to Understanding Art Styles*, Game Developer):**
   Props read as "placed on top" when floor seams are razor-sharp. Projecting localized grime, rust streaks, concrete cracks, water puddles, and scorch marks around building footings, container bases, cover corners, and burning barrels anchors geometry into the terrain/slabs (`Size.z <= 0.22 m` to prevent thin-wall bleed-through).
3. **Competitive Cover-Height & Readability Preservation (Valve / GDC Level Design Metrics & LR2 `B1` / `B46`):**
   Every replaced tactical cover block (`Saltworks` MidYard `ConcreteBlock` 0.90 m, `Deadsector` `StreetCoverN/S` 1.04 m, `TestRange` `MantleTestBox` 0.85 m & `TacticalBlock` 1.08–2.30 m, `Foundry` `CatCoverCrate` 0.90 m) retains its exact collider height and footprint so `GenerateCoverPoints`, mantle metrics, NavMesh carving, and sightlines remain deterministic.
