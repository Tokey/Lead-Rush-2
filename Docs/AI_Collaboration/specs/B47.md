# B47: Recoil model and balance

## Research basis

Recoil is a momentum problem, not a calibre label. A useful first-order estimate is
`impulse = projectile mass * muzzle velocity + gas impulse`; the shooter's measured
recoil energy also depends on firearm mass, stock geometry and muzzle device. The
values below are engineering targets for a 3.5 kg rifle, 1.0 kg pistol and ordinary
service ammunition, not claims that every load has one exact impulse.

Sources:

- [US Army Small Arms Identification and Operations](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/policy/army/fm/3-23-35/index.html) (weapon and employment context).
- [SAAMI cartridge and chamber drawings](https://saami.org/technical-information/cartridge-chamber-drawings/) (standard cartridge dimensions and pressure context).
- [Sniper Country cartridge comparison](https://snipercountry.com/5-56-vs-7-62/) (comparative projectile momentum/recoil discussion).
- [GameDeveloper: weapon feel and recoil](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-art-of-screen-shake) (communicating impact without making control arbitrary).

These are relative tuning anchors. They intentionally do not directly become camera
degrees: weapon mass, grip and stock turn impulse into muzzle rise.

## Fairness rules

1. Use a mostly deterministic pattern (the first 6-10 shots are learnable) with a
   small seeded lateral perturbation of at most 10% after shot 3. Do not use
   frame-rate-dependent random camera jumps.
2. Recovery begins 0.10 s after the shot and returns 80% of accumulated climb in
   0.30 s, with the remaining 20% in 0.55 s. Holding fire should not fully reset.
3. ADS uses the table multiplier; it reduces view displacement, not bullet damage or
   spread to zero. First-shot accuracy gets a 0.04 degree cone in ADS and 0.08
   degrees while hip-fired before bloom.
4. Preserve current TTK parity: automatic rifles should win sustained exposure, the
   pistol should win a deliberate close-range first shot, and FAL damage must be
   paid for with controllability and a 20-round magazine. Re-test 100 HP TTK at
   10/25/40 m after changing damage.
5. A skilled player can counter-pull the pattern; a novice can burst at 4-6 rounds.
   The recoil signal must be visible but never obscure the target for a single shot.

Acceptance: Claude records pre/post 10-shot vertical displacement, ADS/hip values,
recovery-to-80% time, and 100 HP TTK for every row above.
